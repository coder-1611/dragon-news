# Dragon News — thesis

**Soul line:** *Dragon Nation gets its news here.*

## The one image
A 1913 broadsheet coming off a machine built in 2026. Hot-metal bones (blackletter masthead, Didone headlines, column rules, drop caps, halftone photographs, newsprint) set with visible digital precision: a monospace wire layer, a live press clock and headline crawl, registration marks, and a halftone that is actually computed on a canvas. Every "tech" element is something a newsroom really has (wire feed, press time, edition number, registration marks). No sci-fi decoration.

## Direction roll (impeccable concept-seed key 53f0cef5, mode persuade)
Grounded candidates from the RRHS student's world, ranked by resonance (the plain broadsheet front page is the category rut and is excluded from the list):
1. The hot-metal composing room / AP wire room: paste-up board, registration marks, column rules, teletype datelines. **Brief-pinned** ("old school but techy newspaper").
2. Friday-night-lights stadium scoreboard: maroon LED matrix, big tabular numerals, a live crawl.
3. The 1994-95 RRHS CD-ROM yearbook (a real first): mid-90s multimedia interface, pixel type, a disc.
4. The AP wire teletype: continuous perforated paper, mono caps, bell codes.
5. **Assigned by the roll:** the Dragon News broadcast control room: lower-thirds, LIVE bug, timecode, tally lights, program monitor grid.
6. The hallway bulletin board: layered flyers, staples, tape.
7. The Dragon Band drill chart: dotted field grid, yard lines, set numbers.

The user's brief pins the newspaper, so candidate 1 is built. Raises taken from the assigned card and the hand:
- **Raise (control room, #5):** a live press clock and an ON-PRESS status bug in the masthead strip, and the headline crawl is built as a broadcast lower-third, not a marquee.
- **Raise (scoreboard, #2):** edition and issue numerals set huge and tabular; numbers are a display element, not metadata.
- **Raise (CD-ROM yearbook, #3):** the About page tells the true 1994-95 story; the tech lineage is the school's own.
- Challengers dealt by the seed (paper automata, provenance ribbon, cracktro queue, phosphor terminal, gothic-lolita mook, obi sleeve): all **declined** on audience identification; kept from the phosphor terminal the discipline that status prints itself as text rather than appearing as chrome (newsroom statuses are typeset words, never colored pills alone).

## Type
- Masthead: **Grenze Gotisch** (variable blackletter). The American newspaper nameplate vernacular; a dragon paper earns blackletter.
- Headlines: **Bodoni Moda** (variable, opsz 6-96). Didone headline heritage; huge at opsz 96, tight leading 0.92-1.0.
- Body and deks: **Source Serif 4** (variable, opsz). Generous 1.6 body leading, italic deks.
- Metadata and the whole newsroom UI: **Geist Mono**. Folio line, datelines, bylines, kickers, crawl, clock, chips. Tabular numerals everywhere.

## Signature element
The halftone hero: the lead cover rendered as a maroon dot-screen on newsprint by a canvas, resolving toward the photograph under the cursor / on scroll. Registration marks in the corners. Reduced motion: static mid-resolution screen.
