About the paper

Dragon News is the student newspaper of Round Rock High School, written and edited by students for the 3,900 Dragons who walk Deep Wood Drive every morning.

Round Rock High School has been telling its own story since 1913. Dragon News carries that on in print form on the web: a dated edition with a front page, a lead story chosen by the editor, and sections for news, sports, academics, clubs, arts, opinion and Dragon life.

How the paper is made

Student journalists write in the newsroom and submit their stories to the editor. The editor reads every piece, sends back notes, and when a story is ready, accepts it. On press day the editor lays out an edition, picks the lead, orders the sections and publishes. Every edition is kept in the archive exactly as it appeared, so later corrections never quietly rewrite the record.

Old school, new machine

This school has form here. The 1994–95 Round Rock yearbook is believed to be the first ever released on CD-ROM, with 2,000 photographs, 25 minutes of video and 20 minutes of audio. Dragon News keeps that spirit: a newspaper in its bones, built with today's tools.

Join us

Any Dragon can apply for a press pass. Sign up in the newsroom, and once the editor approves your account you can file your first story.
